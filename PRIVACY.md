# Public Corpus Privacy Policy

This repository is public. Its contents must be public-source material that may be redistributed or genuinely original synthetic material.

## Prohibited material

Never copy, adapt, paraphrase, encode, or publish material from a private user or organization project, including:

- project, customer, organization, product, mission, component, or person names;
- local, network, cloud, repository, package, or account paths and identifiers;
- Graphcal source text, comments, file organization, or distinctive model structure;
- input values, datasets, constants, assumptions, or parameter combinations; and
- evaluated results, diagnostics, snapshots, charts, or derived outputs.

Renaming identifiers, changing units, perturbing numbers, removing comments, or asking an agent to "anonymize" private material does not make it suitable for this corpus. Do not use private projects as templates or seeds for synthetic cases.

Do not commit credentials, access tokens, private URLs, personal information, telemetry, or confidential source citations.

## Allowed origins

A contribution must be traceable to one of:

- a cited public source whose relevant inputs may lawfully be redistributed; or
- an original synthetic scenario created independently of private project content.

When reproducing a public source, include only the minimum redistributable data needed for the analysis. Cite rather than copy protected prose, figures, and substantial tables. Record adaptations so reviewers can distinguish source facts from original work.

## Required review

Before opening a pull request, inspect all project files, metadata, expected output, references, and commit content. State the candidate's origin and confirm that the prohibited categories above are absent. Coding-agent generation and automated secret or pattern scanning are only aids; a human privacy review is mandatory.

If potentially private material is discovered, stop sharing it publicly and contact the repository maintainers through a private channel. Do not preserve suspected material in a public issue, pull-request comment, or replacement commit.
