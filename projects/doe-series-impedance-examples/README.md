# DOE series-impedance examples

This source-backed reproduction asks: **Does complex phasor addition reproduce three series-circuit impedance magnitudes printed in the U.S. Department of Energy Electrical Science handbook?**

The project adds feature coverage absent from the earlier corpus: custom electrical dimensions, dimension-aware complex quantities, rectangular and polar forms, magnitude and phase, complex division and conjugation, constrained electrical inputs, a required dimension port, and three reusable DAG instances.

The source pages, sign convention, source precision, and assertion mapping are documented in [`reference/calculation.md`](reference/calculation.md).
